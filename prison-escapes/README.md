# The Greatest Prison Escapes (9:16 story short)

A 63-second vertical story in the 2.5D stickman style: flat outlined characters with big white heads and noodle
limbs in vibrant, textured 3D sets with depth of field. It retells three real escapes:

- **Alcatraz, June 1962**: Frank Morris and John and Clarence Anglin dug out with sharpened spoons, left fake heads
  (soap, toilet paper, real hair) in their beds and left on a raft made from more than fifty raincoats. Never found.
- **El Chapo, 2015**: a 1.5 km tunnel from his shower, with lights, air pipes and a motorbike on rails; recaptured in 2016.
- **Pascal Payet**: escaped from French prisons by helicopter in 2001 and again in 2007.

`build.sh` runs `narration.py` (Kokoro TTS), `music.py`, `subs.py`, renders `scene.js` with `render.mjs` and encodes `greatest-prison-escapes.mp4`.
