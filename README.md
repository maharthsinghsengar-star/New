# Last Train from Bhootpur 🚂👻

A top-down survival-horror game set in a haunted Indian railway station — **Bhootpur Junction** — at night.
Single file, no dependencies: just open `index.html` in a browser (desktop or phone).

## The goal
It's 11:15 PM. The main gate is chained shut. The only way out is the **Moksha Express**, which leaves
**Platform 4 at 11:59 PM**. Miss it and the station keeps you. You have **5 chances**.

To escape you must:
1. Find a **torch** (Waiting Hall)
2. Find the **Ticket Office key** on Platform 1 → take a **ticket**
3. Find a **crowbar** on Platform 2/3 → pry open the **Parcel Room** → **Station Master's key**
4. Search the **Station Master's office** → **footbridge gate key** + **signal lever handle**
5. Pull the **signal lever** (west end of Platform 1) — the train won't come in on red
6. Unlock the **footbridge gate**, cross to Platform 4, and **board** before 11:59

Getting caught costs a chance and 3 minutes. Missing the train costs a chance and restarts the night.

## The haunting
| Threat | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Chudail** (woman in white) | Wanders; sees torchlight from far away; fast once she spots you |
| **The dead TTE** | Patrols all platforms; blows his whistle and alerts every ghost; won't let you board without a ticket |
| **Sirkata Coolie** | Headless porter. Slow, but always knows where you are |
| **Pishach** | Blind; hunts by sound — running is deadly near it |
| **Baccha Bhoot** | Child ghost that only moves when your torch isn't on her |
| **Daayan** | Appears out of nowhere; hold your torch on her to banish her |
| **Vetaal** | Hangs from the footbridge/roof and drops when you pass under |
| **Kaala Kutta** (hell-hounds) | Pack dogs that follow your **scent**, bark to alert ghosts and sniff you out behind benches |
| **Poltergeist** | Unseen; knocks things over (noise), kills lights, whispers |
| **Phantom Express** | A ghost train that tears down Track 1 — don't be on the rails |

## Tools
🔦 Torch (limited battery) · 🔋 Batteries · 💧 Holy Ganga Jal (banish nearby ghosts/dogs) ·
🍪 Biscuits (distract dogs) · 📿 Rudraksha amulet (survive one catch) · 📜 Diary pages (hints on each ghost)

## Hiding
- **Behind benches / chairs / crates** — breaks line of sight, but anything that saw you hide will find you, and dogs will sniff you out.
- **Inside stalls & booths** (Chai, Book, Puri, Cold-drink, STD booth, Enquiry, steel almirah) — real safety, but if something searches nearby, **hold your breath** (Space) without running out of air.

## Controls
WASD/Arrows move · Shift run · Mouse aim torch · F torch · E pick up/use/hide · Space hold breath ·
Q throw biscuits · R holy water · M map · P/Esc pause. On phones, on-screen joystick and buttons appear.

Three difficulties: Yatri (easy), Normal, Nightmare. Headphones recommended.
