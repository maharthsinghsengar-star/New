LAST TRAIN FROM BHOOTPUR

भूतपुर जंक्शन · रात 11:15 · 3D

You missed your connection and fell asleep in the waiting hall of Bhootpur Junction. You wake to flickering tube lights, a dead silence, and the main gate chained shut from outside. The station clock reads 11:15 PM. Something is walking on Platform 2. Something else is howling.

The only way out is the Moksha Express — the last train, leaving Platform 4 at 11:59 PM. Miss it and the station keeps you forever. You have 5 chances.
